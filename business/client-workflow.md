# Client Workflow

Every client gets a GitHub Issue made with the **"New client job"** template. Move it across the project board as the job progresses.

1. **Lead.** An inquiry comes in from the website, DM or a referral. Open an issue and reply within 1 business day.
2. **Quote.** Send a package or custom quote. For drone jobs, check the airspace right away (Aloft/B4UFLY).
3. **Booked.** The client pays the deposit through the website or an invoice. Confirm the date, location and shot list by email.
4. **Prep.** Run through `shoot-checklist.md` the day before. For drone jobs, file LAANC if needed and get the property owner's permission.
5. **Shoot.**
6. **Edit.** Aim to deliver within 7 days.
7. **Deliver.** Send the online gallery (Pixieset, Pic-Time or Google Drive) along with the final invoice.
8. **Website build** (full package only). Draft → client review → launch → 30 days of tweaks.
9. **Paid & closed.** Ask for a Google review and permission to use the photos in the portfolio. Close the issue.

# WCphotography Brand Guide

## Name
**WCphotography**: one word, with "WC" capitalized and "photography" in lowercase. On the website the logo reads **WC**photography, with "photography" in amber.

## Logo
The mark is a **WC monogram inside a lens ring**, with a short amber arc in the top-right for "golden hour" light. Files are in `assets/images/logo/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` / `logo.png` | Full logo (mark + WCphotography) on dark backgrounds |
| `logo-light.svg` / `logo-light.png` | Full logo on light backgrounds |
| `logo-mark.svg` / `logo-mark.png` | Mark only, on dark (profile pictures, watermarks) |
| `logo-mark-light.svg` / `logo-mark-light.png` | Mark only, on light |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | Browser tab and phone home-screen icons |

Use the SVGs wherever you can, since they stay sharp at any size. The PNGs have transparent backgrounds. Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as the amber arc, and don't stretch, recolor or add effects.

## Tagline
> Photos that make your business look as good as it is.

## Colors
| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background (near-black) | `#0E0F11` |
| Surface / cards | `#1D2024` |
| Text (warm white) | `#F2F0EB` |
| Muted text | `#A3A29D` |
| Accent ("golden hour" amber) | `#E0A458` |

These are set at the top of `assets/css/styles.css`.

## Fonts
- Headings: **Playfair Display** (Google Fonts)
- Body: **Inter** (Google Fonts)

## Voice
Friendly, confident and plainspoken. Talk about what the client gets (more customers, a better first impression) rather than gear specs.

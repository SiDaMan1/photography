## Legal notes for drone work (US)
- **Commercial drone flights require an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate** for whoever is flying. Get this before taking paid drone jobs.
- Register each drone used commercially with the FAA (FAADroneZone) and comply with Remote ID.
- Controlled airspace needs a LAANC authorization (instant through apps like Aloft or Autel Explorer).
- FPV goggles count as flying without direct line of sight, so a **visual observer** must be with the pilot.
- Get drone liability insurance (for example SkyWatch or Verifly per-flight, or an annual policy).

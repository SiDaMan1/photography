# Shoot Day Checklist

## Night before
- [ ] Camera batteries charged (plus spares)
- [ ] Memory cards formatted (plus spares)
- [ ] Drone and goggle batteries charged
- [ ] Props inspected, spare props packed
- [ ] Firmware is current (don't update the morning of)
- [ ] Weather and wind checked
- [ ] Airspace checked / LAANC filed
- [ ] Shot list printed or on phone
- [ ] Address, parking and on-site contact confirmed

## Gear
- [ ] Camera bodies and lenses
- [ ] Tripod
- [ ] Lights / reflector / backdrop (product shoots)
- [ ] FPV drone(s), goggles, controller
- [ ] Visual observer assigned for FPV flights
- [ ] Part 107 certificate and drone registration on hand
- [ ] Landing pad, first aid kit, fire-safe LiPo bag

## On site
- [ ] Walk the location with the client
- [ ] Safety brief and clear the flight area of bystanders
- [ ] Get every shot on the shot list, plus extras
- [ ] Back up cards before leaving (or same night)
